# Chennai specialist service content research

Research date: 10 September 2026. Scope: the five main city service pages for building covering, terrace, swimming pool, cricket practice and football/sports nets. Content implementation: `app/content/city-services/specialist.ts`.

## Method and limits

Reviewed current web search results for Chennai service providers and checked safety distinctions against primary guidance and manufacturer information. Search results vary with location, personalisation and time; this review does not claim the sampled sites occupy a verified first-page Google position, and the new content cannot guarantee a search ranking. Competitor facts, rates, warranties, credentials and testimonials were not adopted as DK Safety Solutions claims.

The finished copy is original. Competitor pages informed the questions visitors are trying to answer; the site-specific assessment checklists and comparative scopes were written for this project. No quotations or competitor statistics appear in the service content. Foreign safety sources are used for general distinctions and operating principles, not as evidence of Indian legal requirements or DK product compliance.

## Primary and manufacturer sources

- [HSE: safety nets and soft landing systems](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/safetytopics/safety-nets.htm): personnel fall-arrest nets are specialised energy-absorbing systems requiring competent installation and assessment. Supports the distinction from ordinary facade mesh; no particular fall-arrest capability is claimed for DK.
- [HSE: scaffolds](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/safetytopics/scaffoldinginfo.htm): adding sheeting or debris netting needs coordination with the scaffold designer/supplier. Supports involving the responsible designer before covering an existing scaffold.
- [HSE: health and safety in roof work](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg33.pdf): identifies additional wind loading from debris netting and distinguishes the hazards of debris in a net. Used for assessment boundaries, not as a local regulatory claim.
- [CPSC: drowning prevention](https://www.cpsc.gov/safety-education/neighborhood-safety-network/toolkits/drowning-prevention): supervision and multiple protective measures belong together. Supports the pool page's opening emphasis and separation of netting from wider pool management.
- [CPSC: residential pool barrier guidelines](https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/SafetyBarrierGuidelinesPub362_0423.pdf): barriers do not replace supervision; its guidance is not itself a mandatory standard. The new content does not apply US barrier dimensions or legal rules to Chennai.
- [Katchakid: pool safety net and debris cover](https://katchakid.com/pool-nets-pool-leaf-cover/): manufacturer separates a purpose-designed safety product from a leaf/debris cover and describes complete removal before swimming. The new page applies that distinction without advertising Katchakid products or adopting their performance, certification or handling-time claims.
- [Katchakid: pool net FAQ](https://katchakid.com/pool-nets-faq/): illustrates the need to assess complete products, perimeter details and pool shape. No manufacturer load figures, standards, warranties, prices or product availability were transferred to DK.
- [Huck: specialist net installation](https://www.huck-net.co.uk/pages/hucktek/): distinguishes ball stops, dividers, cricket installations and overhead protection. Supports identifying the actual use and complete system when comparing sports net scopes.
- [Huck: sports netting](https://www.huck-net.co.uk/sports/): separate sports products establish that the broad label sports net is not a universal specification. No particular mesh dimension or twine grade was prescribed in the page.
- [ECB: cricket surfaces and facilities](https://www.ecb.co.uk/news/74645/cricket-surface-types): treats surface quality and facility planning as part of the complete practice environment. The new content separates netting scope from playing-surface work without implying an approved facility.
- [ECB: covered outdoor cricket facility guide, v1.1](https://resources.ecb.co.uk/ecb/document/2025/06/12/46e31b09-bd32-4531-8c09-b165dfbb284e/Covered-Outdoor-Cricket-Facilities-Design-Guide-v1.1.pdf): supports considering net movement, rebound, lane separation and storage. The new content offers no DIY dimensions or site-independent tensioning recipe and does not apply this facility standard to every Chennai practice site.
- [The FA: goalpost safety](https://www.thefa.com/get-involved/goalpost-safety): goals require assembly and securing according to manufacturer instructions, and users must not climb them. Supports the clear distinction between replacing a goal net and assessing goal stability.
- [The FA: field of play](https://www.thefa.com/football-rules-governance/lawsandrules/laws/football-11-11/law-1---the-field-of-play): net support must not interfere with the goalkeeper and goals require secure installation. No match dimensions or competition-compliance claims are included in the DK copy.

## Editorial checks

Each service has a unique city-specific title and description, introduction, overview, four application-specific deep sections, three comparison options, four quotation factors, five enquiry checklist items, four planning steps, three local planning paragraphs, eight answered questions and three related service references. Local neighbourhood examples describe what an enquiry should supply; they are not invented project histories or claims about a particular property's structure.

The specialist pages do not invent prices, credentials, product grades, certification, fixed installation times, warranty periods or client examples. The pool and construction text intentionally avoids unsupported life-safety performance claims. Technical dimensions and final specification are referred to an appropriate site assessment.
